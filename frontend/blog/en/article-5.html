Creating Touching Speeches and Letters in 10 Seconds with AI

Author: Ryu Jae-kuk | AI & Poem Editorial Team
Published: March 24, 2025 | Last updated: April 5, 2026

Among the requests that come into AI & Poem, a surprisingly large portion involves toasts, letters, and thank-you messages. In my experience, a significant share of total usage falls into this "practical poetry" category. One user sent an urgent request — "Tomorrow is my father's 60th birthday and I need to write a congratulatory poem" — followed by a heartfelt thank-you message after receiving the result. Another user shared their experience of writing an anniversary letter with AI assistance. These real-world cases showed me how profoundly AI poetry can serve life's most important moments.

There are moments in life when we need to express our feelings in words. Whether it's a wedding speech for a friend, a thank you note for a parent's 70th birthday, or an anniversary letter to a loved one. However, when we finally pick up the pen, our minds often go blank. In such times, AI & Poem becomes a reliable ally.

1. Wedding Speech: Adding Wit to Sincerity

A speech that is too stiff or too light can be problematic. Try asking the AI, "A wedding speech for my best friend, touching yet humorous." By adding a spoonful of memories with your friend (e.g., "the nights we stayed up studying for exams together"), you can create the perfect speech.

Having used it directly, I've found that the key to a great toast is "relational specificity." When users enter just "friend's wedding toast," they get a generic speech applicable to anyone. But entering "best friend of 10 years, memories of our backpacking trip together" produces a toast uniquely tailored to that relationship.

2. Love Letter: Between Cheesy and Romantic

Instead of just repeating "I love you," try borrowing poetic expressions. Instead of the common phrase "You are my world," the AI can suggest something like "You are the only starlight that fills my universe." As long as you take the time to write the generated poem by hand, it will be enough to capture your partner's heart.

Analyzing AI & Poem usage data, the elements users most frequently modify in love letter outputs are "terms of endearment" and "shared memories." They keep the AI's poetic structure but add places and episodes only the two of them know. This is when the letter's emotional impact doubles — a perfect example of human-AI collaborative writing.

3. Thank You Note: With Graceful Sentences

On Teacher's Day or Parents' Day, there are times when you want to express your gratitude but can't find the right words. Try generating a poem that conveys "respect and gratitude." A message with refined language and rhythm will resonate deeply with the recipient.

Usage Tip: Use the Reading Function

Once you've finished writing, use the 'Read Aloud' feature of AI & Poem to listen to it beforehand. You can check if there are any awkward parts when read aloud and if the emotions are conveyed well. Recording yourself reading it and sending the audio file can also be a special gift.

In my experience, the area where AI excels most in thank-you messages is "balancing formality with sincerity." Whether addressing a retiring boss, a graduating student, or a long-time mentor, AI establishes the appropriate tone while users contribute the specific reasons for their gratitude. This combination consistently produces the highest satisfaction.

What AI Does Best vs. What Only You Can Add

After analyzing thousands of toast and letter generation results from AI & Poem, I've identified a clear pattern: AI excels at "structure and rhetoric," while humans excel at "context and authenticity." Adding just one line of specific shared memory on top of AI's beautifully crafted sentences transforms the text into something truly yours.

As AI technology advances, ceremonial writing will become increasingly accessible to everyone. But what breathes life into these words will always be the writer's heart. AI & Poem aims to be both a tool and a companion in translating your feelings into the most beautiful words possible.
