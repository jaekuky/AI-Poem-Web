The Principle of AI Writing Poetry: The Meeting of Technology and Emotion

Author: Ryu Jae-kuk | AI & Poem Editorial Team
Published: March 24, 2025 | Last updated: April 5, 2026

Planning and developing the AI & Poem service, I read hundreds of AI-generated poems every day. Most of them are technically proficient — well-structured, with smooth rhythm and appropriate metaphors. But one day, a poem on the theme of 'loneliness' stopped me in my tracks. The line "even silence has weight on nights without you" felt genuinely moving. That experience prompted me to dig deeper into how AI actually creates poetry, and what it means when a machine-generated verse resonates with us emotionally. This article shares what I've learned from that journey.

Recently, with the advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) technology, the realm of creation has expanded to include art, music, and literature. Services like 'AI & Poem' can generate poetry on any topic the user desires in just a few seconds. So, how can the cold machine of AI mimic the 'emotion' that was once considered the exclusive domain of humans?

The Magic of Large Language Models (LLMs)

At the core of AI's ability to write poetry is the **Large Language Model (LLM)**. Models like GPT have been trained on billions of sentences and text data. In this process, AI learns not just to memorize words, but to understand the probabilistic relationships between words, context, and nuance.

For example, it statistically understands that words like "flower," "warmth," and "sprout" are likely to follow the word "spring." However, poetry is not just a game of probabilities; it requires metaphor and symbolism.

Pattern Recognition and Creativity

AI identifies poetic patterns within the learned data. By analyzing tens of thousands of poems, it learns structures such as rhyme, rhythm, and poetic license. When a user presents the theme of "the sorrow of parting," AI connects emotional vocabulary related to that theme and rearranges sentences according to the learned poetic structure.

  • Association: It associates words like 'rain,' 'sea,' and 'cloudiness' with the word 'tear.'
  • Structuring: It adopts forms like the narrative structure or a four-line poem.
  • Style Imitation: If requested to mimic a specific poet's style, it emulates their characteristics (e.g., use of archaic language, short breaths).

Inside the Engine: Transformer Architecture and Self-Attention

The magic behind modern AI poetry lies in the Transformer architecture. At its core is the self-attention mechanism, which allows the model to weigh the importance of every word in relation to every other word in a sentence. When generating a line like "the autumn wind whispers through empty hallways," the model simultaneously considers the relationships between "autumn," "wind," "whispers," "empty," and "hallways" — understanding that "whispers" connects both to "wind" (the actor) and "empty hallways" (the atmosphere).

In my experience developing AI & Poem, the temperature parameter is one of the most fascinating controls. At low temperatures (around 0.3), AI produces predictable, conventional poetry. At high temperatures (above 1.0), it generates wild, sometimes incoherent imagery. The sweet spot for most users falls between 0.7 and 0.9 — creative enough to surprise, structured enough to make sense. This is where AI poetry finds its unique voice.

From Input to Verse: The 4-Step Generation Process

When a user enters a theme like "autumn evening" into AI & Poem, four things happen in sequence. First, the input is tokenized — broken into meaningful units the model can process. Second, the model searches its learned patterns for associations: autumn connects to falling leaves, fading light, nostalgia. Third, it generates candidate lines, scoring each for coherence, rhythm, and emotional resonance. Finally, it selects and arranges the best lines into a structured poem. Having used this system directly, I've observed that the quality of the input theme dramatically affects each of these stages.

Limitations and Possibilities of AI Poetry

Of course, AI does not actually feel 'sorrow' or 'joy.' It is the result of meticulous calculations. However, if that result moves the reader, can we not call it art? AI can present unique combinations of words that human writers may not have considered, becoming a wonderful tool that breaks through the barriers of creativity.

Looking Ahead: The Future of AI Poetry

Having built and operated AI & Poem, I believe we are at the beginning of a profound shift in creative expression. AI will not replace human poets — but it is already expanding who can participate in poetry. People who never considered themselves poets are discovering their voice through AI collaboration. The technology will continue to evolve, but the fundamental truth remains: the most powerful poems are born where human experience meets linguistic craft. AI can provide the craft; only we can provide the experience.
