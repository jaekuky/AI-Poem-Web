About the Author

About the Creator

Ryu Jae-kuk is the creator and developer of AI & Poem. By combining artificial intelligence technology with literature, he is building a platform for poetic communication that transcends language and borders.

Career and Background

With hands-on experience in software development, he integrates Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Large Language Model (LLM) technologies into the service. He pursues both the potential of technology and the beauty of literature, focusing on designing user-centered creative tools.

Expertise in AI and Literature

AI & Poem is a multilingual poetry generation service supporting 32 languages, with deep attention to prompt design that considers the poetic traditions and cultural contexts of each language. The service continuously improves its technical sophistication through transformer-based poetry generation, TTS (text-to-speech) integration, and content safety filtering.

Service Philosophy

Built on the belief that poetry should be open to everyone, AI & Poem lowers the barriers to creative writing and helps more people express emotions and communicate through poetry. Even those with no writing experience can start with a single word, using AI-generated poems as a starting point to find their own voice.
