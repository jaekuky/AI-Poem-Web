Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 22, 2026

AI & Poem ("Service", "we", "us") provides an AI poetry generation tool at https://ai-and-poem.art. The Service is operated by Ryu Jae-kuk. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and how we protect it. By accessing or using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to operate the Service and comply with Google AdSense policies.

  • Automatically collected (server logs). Cloudflare Pages (frontend hosting) and AWS CloudWatch (backend Lambda logs) automatically record IP addresses, User-Agent strings, timestamps, Referer, and request paths as infrastructure logs.
  • Voluntarily submitted (poem topic). The poem topic you type (up to 200 characters) is used solely to generate the requested poem and is not stored in the backend after we send the response. We do not operate any database or object storage (stateless Lambda).
  • Cookies and local storage. We store consent state in browser local storage under the key aiAndPoemCookieConsent (a JSON object with essential/analytics/advertising consent flags). Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-7349192768683799) and Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-1CVHJYZWNC) may also set cookies and similar technologies under their own policies.

2. Collection Method

  • Automatic collection. Infrastructure logs (IP / User-Agent / timestamp) and cookies set by advertising and analytics partners are collected automatically when pages load and when ads or events fire.
  • Voluntary submission. The poem topic text is collected only when you fill in the input form and press the submit button.
  • We do not separately collect identifying information such as name, email, or phone number on the client side.

3. How We Use Information

  • To generate the AI poems you request and return the response.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Service and perform statistical analysis.
  • To serve ads, measure ads, and personalize ads through Google AdSense.
  • To comply with our Terms of Service and applicable laws, and to prevent abuse.

4. Third-Party Sharing and Processors

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share or entrust data with the third parties listed below to operate the Service.

  • Google AdSense — ad delivery, measurement, and personalization (publisher ID ca-pub-7349192768683799). See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Google Analytics 4 — traffic analytics (measurement ID G-1CVHJYZWNC).
  • OpenAI, L.L.C. — your poem topic is forwarded to OpenAI's GPT API to generate the poem. Servers are located in the United States.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — Cloudflare Pages static hosting and global CDN.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS). — AWS Lambda backend (Seoul region ap-northeast-2) and CloudWatch logs.

5. Data Retention

Your poem topic and the generated poem are discarded as soon as the request is fulfilled; we do not store them in any database or object storage. Infrastructure access logs (Cloudflare, AWS CloudWatch) are retained according to each provider's retention policy.

6. International Transfers

The Service relies on global cloud infrastructure, so data may be processed in multiple countries. Primary processing locations include:

  • Cloudflare global edge network.
  • AWS Lambda Seoul region (ap-northeast-2).
  • OpenAI API (United States).

We apply reasonable technical and administrative safeguards consistent with applicable laws and contractual requirements.
