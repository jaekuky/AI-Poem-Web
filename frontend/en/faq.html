Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about AI & Poem. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us.

Getting Started

Is AI & Poem free to use?

Yes, it is completely free. No sign-up or payment required — start generating poems instantly.

Do I need to create an account or log in?

No account is needed. Simply enter a topic on the main page and generate your poem with one click.

How long does it take to generate a poem?

Most poems are ready within 10 seconds, including the voice narration — ready to enjoy and share immediately.

Features

What languages are supported?

We support over 32 languages including English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, and French. Curious about multilingual poetry? Read Writing Poetry in 32 Languages.

How do I use the voice narration feature?

After a poem is generated, click the 'Read Poem' button to hear it read aloud in your selected language with natural-sounding speech.

Can I create a poem on any topic?

Yes — love, nature, seasons, farewells, travel, and more. Content involving violence, hate speech, or adult themes is restricted.

Can I choose a specific style or format?

Yes. Pick a poem form (free verse, haiku, sonnet, or acrostic) from the 'Poem form' selector above the topic box. For mood, add words like 'lyrical' or 'short and powerful' to your topic and the AI will match them.

Can I save or share the generated poem?

You can copy the poem and share it freely via social media, messaging apps, or email. Visit our Gallery to explore more poem examples.

AI & Creativity

How does AI create poetry?

A large language model (LLM) analyzes the topic and language you provide, then crafts a poem with rhythm, emotion, and structure. Learn more in Principles and Future of AI Poem Generators.

Is every generated poem unique?

Yes. Even with the same topic, each generation produces a fresh, original poem — AI has virtually infinite creative variation.

How does AI poetry compare to human-written poetry?

AI draws on vast literary data to produce poetry quickly, but it complements rather than replaces the depth of human experience. Explore the history in The Day Computers Started Writing Poetry.

Copyright & Use

Can I use the generated poems commercially?

Yes. You can use them in campaigns, newsletters, events, and more. Please credit AI & Poem when possible.

Who owns the copyright of AI-generated poems?

The copyright ownership of AI-generated works is a legally complex and evolving issue that varies by country. Read Who Owns AI-Written Poetry? for a detailed discussion.

Can it be used for educational purposes?

Absolutely. It is well suited for creative writing classes, language learning, and emotional expression exercises. See how in AI Poets in the Classroom.

Can AI poetry be used for emotional healing?

Yes. AI-generated poems can serve as a therapeutic tool for people who find it hard to express emotions in words. Learn more in AI Poetry and Emotional Healing.
